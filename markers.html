Printable markers

Print at 100% / Actual size (not "fit to page"). Each QR prints at the size chosen below. Glue it flat and upright on top of the object: the ▲ arrow is the object's facing direction (used for the directional rays).

Size:  

Shield: fold a cardboard wall about 3.4 cm wide × 6 cm tall (game scale on a 13" laptop: 44 m × 80 m) and glue it standing up along the brown "WALL" line, so the QR card lies flat behind the wall and the ▲ arrow points through the wall toward Dracula. The wall offset (0.75 × QR size in front of the QR center) can be tuned in the board's Debug panel.
